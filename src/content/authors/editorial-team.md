Articles under the Editorial Team byline are produced by multiple contributors including our business payments systems editor, payment security editor, and outside fact-checkers. We use this byline when no single author is primarily responsible for a piece — most often on comparison articles, service roundups, and news updates where multiple people contributed research.

Our editorial process for every article:

1. **Research first.** Every claim about a fee, price, or vendor policy is sourced from the vendor's own current pricing page, not from third-party summaries.
2. **Math verification.** Every cost-per-check calculation is independently re-checked before publication.
3. **Honest counter-examples.** Every strategy article must include at least one clear scenario where the recommendation does NOT apply.
4. **Disclosure.** Affiliate relationships are disclosed on every page and in each relevant article.
5. **Regular updates.** Articles are reviewed at least quarterly or whenever we learn a material term has changed.
